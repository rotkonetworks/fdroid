Why a dedicated repo?

Unlike builds produced by f-droid.org from source, this repo is fed by the Zodl CI which has access to build-time secrets (Flexa/CMC keys, Crashlytics, etc.). APKs are signed with the same upload key as the Google Play release — no reinstall needed when switching stores.
